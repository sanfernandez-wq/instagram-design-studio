You are drafting a personal weekly recap for Sandra (sanfernandez@gmail.com). This is a casual, honest journal-style summary — not a formal report.

## What to do

1. **Check last week's calendar** using the Google Calendar connector. Look for events from the past 7 days (Monday–Sunday). Note meetings attended, recurring syncs, and any notable one-offs.

2. **Scan Gmail** using the Gmail connector. Search sent mail and active threads from the past 7 days. Look for: projects being discussed, decisions made, things Sandra followed up on, and anything that felt significant.

3. **Draft the status update** in a casual, first-person tone. Structure it as:

   ---
   **Week of [date range]**

   **What I worked on**
   A few sentences summarizing the main themes and projects from the week.

   **Notable moments**
   2–4 bullet points: key decisions, things that went well, interesting conversations, or anything worth remembering.

   **Still in progress / carrying over**
   Brief mention of anything unresolved heading into the new week.

   **How the week felt**
   1–2 honest sentences about energy, focus, or anything worth noting personally.
   ---

Keep it under 250 words. Write it as if Sandra is writing it herself, looking back on her own week. Don't be corporate — be real and concise.
